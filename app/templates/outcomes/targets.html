What This Form Does

Record the expected deliverables and measurable targets stated in this approved proposal. The outcome evaluation will compare the actual project results against these targets when generating the Low, Medium, or High rating, ensuring the assessment is aligned with this specific proposal.

  • Enter the target value for each measurable indicator and describe the expected deliverable/outcome in the Basis field.
  • Leave a target blank to remove it for that indicator (the system will fall back to its default benchmark).
  • The rating updates immediately on the outcome assessment page once you save.
