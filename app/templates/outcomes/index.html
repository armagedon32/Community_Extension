How the Outcome Rating Is Generated

Each approved extension project (status Ongoing or Completed) is scored by comparing its actual results against the expected deliverables and measurable targets recorded for that specific approved proposal. The system applies clearly defined thresholds, and the rating is displayed together with the expected target, actual result, applicable indicator, and classification criterion.

  • Record targets: the expected deliverables and measurable targets of each approved proposal can be recorded, and update immediately what each indicator measures.
  • Dataset: the evaluation consults projects, activities, accomplishment reports, beneficiaries, approved financial transactions, and partnership records.
  • Indicators: Deliverable Completion, Deliverable Documentation, Activity Implementation, Beneficiary Reach, and Budget Utilization.
  • Attainment = actual / target. Overall score = equal-weighted average of indicator scores (0–100).
  • Rating: Low < 50  |  Medium 50–79  |  High ≥ 80.
  • Proposed projects are still pending approval and are not scored ({{ data.excluded_proposed }} pending).
