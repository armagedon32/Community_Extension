Methodology Notes
  • Durations are actual recorded (timed) observations of each procedure, stored per trial. Averages use the arithmetic mean of the recorded trials for each method.
  • Time difference = manual average − system average. A positive difference means the developed system completes the task faster.
  • % improvement = (manual average − system average) ÷ manual average × 100. This is displayed per indicator and averaged across all fully compared indicators.
  • Perception data: the ISO/IEC 25010 performance–efficiency ratings (1–5 Likert) are user-perception data, displayed separately. They support the evaluation but are not the sole basis for turnaround-time improvement claims.
